# Empirical strategy

This section describes the sample, the timing of observation, and the source of variation used in the analysis.

The outcome is measured at the firm-year level and is reported with the unit and time window stated in the table notes.

The design compares observations exposed to the policy with observations that were not exposed during the same calendar window.

We report the estimand as a conditional association for the observed sample and distinguish it from a population-wide effect.

The specification includes the predeclared covariates and fixed effects described in the data section.

Standard errors are clustered at the level at which treatment timing is assigned.

The results section reports point estimates, uncertainty intervals, sample counts, and the corresponding economic scale.

We examine alternative windows and bandwidths as sensitivity analyses rather than as independent confirmations of the main result.

The identifying variation may not support extrapolation to firms outside the observed support.

All variables retain the definitions used in the data appendix, and table references are kept stable across versions.

The limitations section records measurement error, missingness, and the possibility of residual confounding.

The discussion links the estimates to the research question without treating statistical precision as economic importance.

The appendix provides the coding decisions and additional descriptive checks needed to interpret the estimates.
