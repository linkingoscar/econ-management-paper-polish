# Close Reading (逐文精读)

Use this file for templates and guidelines when doing detailed reading of individual papers. Close reading extracts maximum value from each paper for the research project.

## When To Use

- Reading core papers in detail.
- Preparing to write literature review.
- Extracting specific information for citation.
- User asks to analyze a paper deeply.

## Reading Strategy

### First Pass: Skim (10-15 min)

1. Read abstract
2. Read introduction (first and last paragraphs)
3. Scan section headings
4. Look at tables and figures
5. Read conclusion

**Goal**: Understand what the paper is about and whether it's relevant.

### Second Pass: Structure (20-30 min)

1. Read introduction carefully
2. Read theory/hypotheses section
3. Skim data and method
4. Study main result tables
5. Read discussion/conclusion

**Goal**: Understand the paper's structure and main claims.

### Third Pass: Deep Read (45-60 min)

1. Read every section carefully
2. Check all tables and figures
3. Verify numbers and claims
4. Note strengths and weaknesses
5. Extract key quotes

**Goal**: Full understanding for citation and comparison.

## Extraction Checklist

### For Literature Review
- [ ] How does this paper position itself?
- [ ] What gap does it claim to fill?
- [ ] What literature streams does it contribute to?
- [ ] What is the theoretical framework?

### For Method Section
- [ ] What method is used?
- [ ] What is the identification strategy?
- [ ] What robustness checks are performed?
- [ ] What are the limitations?

### For Theory Section
- [ ] What theory is used?
- [ ] How are constructs defined?
- [ ] What mechanisms are proposed?
- [ ] What boundary conditions are identified?

### For Results Section
- [ ] What are the main findings?
- [ ] How large are the effects?
- [ ] What mechanisms are tested?
- [ ] What heterogeneity is found?
