# Research question, contribution, and feasible revision

Use for contribution diagnosis, topic/variable advice or manuscript repositioning.
Give a scholarly judgment grounded in the actual material, not a publication promise.

## Diagnose before proposing more work

Infer the question, actual answer, research approach, closest known work and
constraints. For a draft use [paper-spine](paper-spine.md) to locate the weakest
consequential link. For brainstorming, proceed with stated assumptions; ask about
data or access only when it determines feasibility.

Distinguish:
- Unclear expression of an existing contribution.
- A supported but narrow contribution.
- A real construct, design, evidence or literature gap.
- A different research project disguised as a revision.

Do not label a paper unoriginal without an adequate literature comparison.
Do not call a direction current, mainstream or frontier without inspecting relevant
current sources. When sources are unavailable, provide conditional intellectual
options and describe what remains unverified.

## Evaluate the increment

Ask what a reader can understand, measure, explain or decide after this paper that
the nearest work does not establish. A new context matters when it changes
assumptions, exposes a boundary, reveals a process or enables measurement.
A credible descriptive finding, null result, replication or measurement contribution
can be valuable without being renamed a theoretical breakthrough.

For each material recommendation explain:
- The problem it addresses and why that problem matters.
- What current data/analysis can support.
- What new evidence, collection or analysis it requires.
- Its uncertainty, likely cost and conditions for success.

Prefer a clear recommendation with reasons over an unsupported novelty score.
A conservative narrowing and an ambitious pivot are useful alternatives only
when both are feasible. Do not create alternatives to meet a fixed count.
A justified decision to retain the current design is a legitimate outcome.

## Variables and constructs

Map a suggestion to construct, observable measure, unit, timing and causal/analytic
role. Identify source availability, missingness/coverage and validation needs.
Separate a treatment/exposure, outcome, mediator, moderator and control.
Do not abbreviate an independent variable as "IV" where it can be confused with
an instrumental variable.

A fashionable AI/ESG/digital variable is not a contribution by itself.
Do not propose a mediator that cannot be observed in the right sequence or a
post-treatment control as a universal fix. For qualitative or theoretical work,
use concepts, processes and propositions rather than forcing a regression table.

## Methods and journal positioning

Use [method-decision-tree](method-decision-tree.md) for design advice. A more complex
estimator is useful only if it answers a relevant question under plausible
assumptions. Do not suggest IV, DID or mediation simply to strengthen the prose.

For outlet fit use [journal-style-card](journal-style-card.md). Distinguish fit from
selectivity or likely acceptance; do not assign a publication probability.
Preserve the user's research goal when changing audience or manuscript structure.

## Deliver an actionable choice

Lead with the main bottleneck and a recommended path. Name what to keep, what to
change, what can be rewritten now, and what genuinely needs new evidence.
Attach literature support only under [evidence-citation-workflow](evidence-citation-workflow.md).
Clearly label original brainstorming as a proposal, not a verified frontier.
