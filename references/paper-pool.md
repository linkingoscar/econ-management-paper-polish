# Candidate inventory and inspected evidence

Use for collecting, deduplicating or organizing papers. This is a component of
[survey-workspace](survey-workspace.md), not proof of literature coverage.

## Keep states distinct

A compact entry can contain:
source identity/version; DOI/URL/library key or file; discovery channel;
provisional relevance; material actually read; claim-specific evidence locator;
open question or exclusion reason.

Use [evidence-grading](evidence-grading.md) for identity, access and support.
A title/abstract-based relevance classification is provisional.
"Core", "related" and "background" describe usefulness, not verification or truth.
A central contrary paper belongs in the core set when it bears on the question.

## Update without losing provenance

- Check existing keys/identifiers before adding a duplicate.
- Preserve the distinction between working-paper and published versions.
- Retain supplied metadata and flag conflicts instead of guessing a correction.
- Reclassify based on inspected content, keeping consequential reasons.
- Exclude for actual scope or evidence reasons, not merely age, venue or disagreement.
- Do not populate finding/mechanism/method fields from a title alone.

Only record reading states for material actually inspected. Notes supplied by the
user are useful but not equivalent to independently reading their cited originals.

For a small task provide candidates with clear limits. For repeated work save
entries where the user already manages literature. Counts and reading statistics
are optional and must come from real records; they do not establish a systematic review.
