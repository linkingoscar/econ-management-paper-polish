# Adjacent Field Style Packs

Use only after the four-quadrant route is chosen. This file refines adjacent or mixed business-school fields that do not fit cleanly into pure economics or pure management.

Primary routes remain:

- Chinese economics: `cn-economics-style.md`.
- English economics: `en-economics-style.md`.
- Chinese management: `cn-management-style.md`.
- English management: `en-management-style.md`.

Use this file to add field-specific constraints for finance, accounting, marketing, IS, operations, public management, tourism/service, and mixed papers.

## Finance

Closest route: economics for asset pricing/markets; management for governance/strategic finance; mixed for corporate finance.

Writing focus:

- Name the market participant: firms, managers, investors, lenders, analysts, auditors, regulators, or intermediaries.
- Clarify the financial channel: information, agency, risk, liquidity, financing, valuation, governance, or disclosure.
- Treat endogeneity and alternative explanations as central.
- Report economic magnitude and market relevance.

Reviewer risks:

- Mechanism is not financial.
- Proxy validity is assumed.
- Results lack market or firm-level interpretation.

## Accounting

Closest route: finance/economics for archival capital-market work; management for managerial/accounting-control topics.

Writing focus:

- Define reporting, audit, taxation, disclosure, internal-control, or information-environment constructs.
- Explain proxy construction and measurement limits.
- Distinguish archival, experimental, survey, and analytical accounting styles.
- Separate construct validity from empirical association.

Reviewer risks:

- Proxy is treated as the latent construct.
- Institutional detail is too thin.
- Causal language exceeds design.

## Marketing

Closest route: management for consumer behavior and strategy; economics for quantitative marketing, platforms, pricing, and market response.

Writing focus:

- For consumer behavior, build psychological mechanism and experimental validity.
- For quantitative marketing, emphasize model assumptions, identification, prediction/decision value, and managerial actionability.
- Interpret results through consumer choice, firm marketing decisions, channels, brands, pricing, or platform behavior.

Reviewer risks:

- Managerial implication is too broad.
- Consumer mechanism is asserted without measurement.
- Model fit is discussed without decision relevance.

## Information Systems

Closest route: management for behavioral/theory IS; economics for platform empirics; design-science route when artifact building is central.

Writing focus:

- Name the IT artifact, digital affordance, platform mechanism, algorithm/data system, or sociotechnical process.
- Explain why the digital setting changes theory, identification, or design value.
- Avoid treating "digital", "AI", or "platform" as a contribution by itself.

Reviewer risks:

- Technology is just a setting.
- Empirical design borrows economics language but target outlet expects IS theory.
- Mechanism is generic management language.

## Operations And Supply Chain

Closest route: management for organizational operations; economics/quantitative route for formal models and market mechanisms.

Writing focus:

- Clarify process, coordination, resilience, quality, risk, efficiency, service operation, or supply-chain mechanism.
- Connect empirical results to operational decisions.
- State model/data assumptions when using optimization, simulation, or analytics methods.

Reviewer risks:

- Operational construct is vague.
- Practical decision relevance is missing.
- Method sophistication is not tied to operations insight.

## Public Management And Policy Management

Closest route: management for public organizations; economics for policy evaluation and public finance.

Writing focus:

- Clarify governance capacity, accountability, incentives, implementation, public value, or policy process.
- Avoid firm-management language unless public organizations are analyzed as organizations.
- Use official policy sources for policy facts.

Reviewer risks:

- Policy commentary substitutes for research question.
- Public-sector context is treated like private firm management.
- Implications exceed evidence.

## Tourism, Hospitality, And Service Management

Closest route: management/marketing for behavior and service experience; economics for regional development and policy evaluation.

Writing focus:

- Explain destination, service, experience, platform-review, sustainability, or hospitality-firm mechanism.
- Keep industry specificity visible.
- Scope external validity carefully.

Reviewer risks:

- Tourism setting is descriptive only.
- Consumer/service mechanism is not measured.
- Policy or managerial implications are generic.

## Mixed Papers

When fields overlap:

1. Choose target outlet first.
2. Choose the dominant contribution logic: identification, theory, market mechanism, construct/mechanism, decision relevance, or policy evaluation.
3. Use the closest quadrant file.
4. Add only the adjacent-field constraints needed for precision.
