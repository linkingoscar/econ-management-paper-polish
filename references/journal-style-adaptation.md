# Field style versus journal compliance

Use for broad audience adaptation after understanding the research approach.
For a named journal's requirements use [journal-style-card](journal-style-card.md).

## Presentation choices

- Chinese economics: make institutional or policy context serve a precise economic
  question. It need not open every paper. State actual findings and their scope.
- English economics: make the economic object, difficulty, contribution and evidence
  intelligible; emphasize identification only where a causal claim calls for it.
- Chinese management: connect organizational phenomena with the relevant conceptual,
  process or empirical question. Do not add theory names or policy slogans as decoration.
- English management: explain constructs, mechanisms or theory building with appropriate
  evidence. Preserve phenomenon-led and qualitative approaches.
- Finance/accounting: foreground relevant market/reporting institutions, measurement
  and comparisons; distinguish archival, analytical and experimental designs.
- Marketing/IS: identify the decision, behavior, model or technology artifact and
  the relevant explanatory/predictive/design contribution.

Use the relevant field file for detail, not every file in the family.
These are editorial heuristics, not verified claims about all journals.

## Adaptation boundary

Preserve the study's supported answer and type of contribution. Style cannot turn
a proxy into a construct, an association into a causal effect, or a local result
into a universal prescription. User-provided sample papers can guide organization
without licensing copying or establishing current rules.

Infer the requested depth and proceed; do not require the user to choose among
imitation modes or declare a journal merely to polish a passage.
Return the revised text and material choices, not a mandatory style-card preamble.
