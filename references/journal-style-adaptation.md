# Journal Style Adaptation

Use this file after classifying the discipline and language context. If the user names a specific journal, prefer `journal-style-card.md` and the journal's current author instructions/recent article style over these generic profiles.

## When To Ask

Ask the user for target outlet only when the answer changes style materially. A concise question is enough:

> 目标期刊或期刊类型是什么？如果还没定，我会按中文 CSSCI 兼容范式或英文细分领域通用范式处理。

If the user has no target journal, use the compatible default for the language and discipline.

## Chinese CSSCI-Compatible Style

Use for Chinese economics and management journals when no specific journal is named.

Typical expectations:

- Strong "problem consciousness": real-world background should quickly become a research question, not remain policy commentary.
- Clear "literature gap" and "possible marginal contribution": specify which literature stream is advanced and how.
- More explicit transition from Chinese context or institutional setting to theoretical significance.
- Literature review often appears as a dedicated section, but it should be thematic rather than a list.
- Empirical sections use formal, restrained wording: "表X报告了...", "结果显示...", "这表明...".
- Policy or managerial implications are expected in many Chinese outlets, but must be scoped to the evidence.

Chinese wording preferences:

- Prefer "本文" over excessive "我们" unless the target journal uses "我们".
- Use "研究发现" or "结果表明" for findings.
- Use "可能的边际贡献在于" only when the contribution is specific.
- Use "政策启示" for economics/public policy and "管理启示" for management/firm decision papers.
- Avoid empty phrases: "具有重要现实意义", "丰富了相关研究", "产生显著影响" without specifying mechanism or evidence.

Representative CSSCI-style outlet families to consider when the user gives a target:

- Economics: economic theory, macro, industrial economics, labor, public finance, development, regional/urban, international trade.
- Management: strategy, organization, innovation, HR, operations, entrepreneurship, public management.
- Finance/accounting: capital markets, corporate finance, audit, disclosure, governance, accounting standards.

Do not claim a specific journal requirement unless it has been checked from current author instructions or recent papers.

## English Field-Journal-Compatible Style

Use when no specific English journal is named.

General principles:

- Lead with the research question, identification/theory challenge, and contribution.
- Use lean, claim-first paragraphs.
- Avoid Chinese-style broad policy openings unless the setting itself is central.
- Avoid generic "theoretical and practical significance"; map contributions to named literature and concrete findings.
- Keep causal language aligned with design.

## English Outlet Families

### General And Applied Economics

Reference style family: top general and field economics journals.

Adaptation:

- Shorter theoretical exposition unless the model is central.
- Clear identification and empirical design language.
- Results paragraphs should emphasize magnitude, interpretation, and robustness.
- Contributions are often framed as what the paper identifies, measures, or explains in a literature.
- Policy implications should be evidence-bound and not managerial in tone.

### Finance

Reference style family: corporate finance, asset pricing, banking, and governance journals.

Adaptation:

- Make the financial channel and market participant explicit.
- Discuss endogeneity, alternative explanations, and economic magnitude early.
- Use precise terms for returns, risk, disclosure, governance, financing, and valuation.

### Accounting

Reference style family: archival, experimental, audit, disclosure, and taxation journals.

Adaptation:

- Explain institutional details and measurement validity.
- Distinguish reporting incentives, information environment, audit quality, and capital market consequences.
- Be careful with proxy language: a proxy is evidence for a construct, not the construct itself.

### Management, Strategy, And Organization

Reference style family: management, strategy, organization theory, OB, HRM, and entrepreneurship journals.

Adaptation:

- Build theory carefully through constructs, mechanisms, and boundary conditions.
- Hypotheses should not read like regression expectations; they should follow from theory.
- Results should return to theory, not only report coefficients.
- Managerial implications should be concrete but not consultancy-style.

### Marketing

Reference style family: marketing strategy, consumer behavior, and quantitative marketing journals.

Adaptation:

- Connect theory or model results to consumer choice, firm decisions, channels, brands, pricing, or platform behavior.
- For behavioral work, emphasize validity of manipulations and psychological mechanism.
- For quantitative work, emphasize model assumptions, identification, predictive or decision relevance, and managerial actionability.

### Information Systems

Reference style family: IS behavioral, economics of IS, digital platforms, and IT management journals.

Adaptation:

- Name the IT artifact, digital affordance, platform mechanism, or sociotechnical process.
- Separate digital context description from theoretical contribution.
- Adapt tone depending on whether the manuscript is theory/behavioral IS or economics-style platform empirics.

## Specific Journal Workflow

When the user names a journal:

1. Ask whether to strictly imitate that journal's style or only make the paper broadly suitable.
2. Check the current author guidelines and 2-3 recent papers if web access is available and the task requires journal-specific conformity.
3. Create a compact style card: section norms, voice, contribution framing, citation style, word/abstract constraints if verified, and reviewer sensitivities.
4. Apply that card to the revision.
5. Mention any unverified assumptions.

Never invent word limits, format requirements, or reviewer preferences.
