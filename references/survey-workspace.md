# A reusable literature workspace

Use for repeated literature work, a substantial survey or a requested research
workspace. Reuse the user's existing folder, library or notes.
A small literature question can be answered without creating a workspace.

## Organize around the question

Keep only useful artifacts:
- Research question, scope, search terms and actual coverage.
- Candidate inventory and inspected evidence, in distinguishable states.
- Focused reading notes for central sources.
- Comparison/synthesis of findings, designs, constructs and disagreements.
- Unresolved questions and feasible next actions.

One document or table may suffice. Create subfolders or separate files only when
the volume or reuse warrants them; do not pre-create an empty template tree.
No vector database or particular note-taking application is required.

## Discovery is not synthesis

Use [paper-pool](paper-pool.md) for candidates and identity/version management,
[close-reading](close-reading.md) for inspected evidence, and
[evidence-citation-workflow](evidence-citation-workflow.md) for citation use.

Compare studies by what they establish and under which conditions.
A timeline can help when genuine intellectual changes are supported by the sources;
do not invent "origin/development/frontier" eras to fill a template.
Methods maps should compare questions, assumptions and evidence needs, not imply
that newer estimators are inherently better.

## Contribution opportunities

Distinguish a gap in this search from a demonstrated unresolved question.
Explain why answering the question would matter and what evidence is feasible.
Include counterexamples, nulls and boundary conditions; do not exclude a paper
because its conclusion is inconvenient.

Use [topic-revision-advisor](topic-revision-advisor.md) to compare feasible options.
Keep initial ideas and hypotheses provisional. Do not manufacture expected results
or aggregate star ratings to justify a direction.

## Resume without repeating work

Record source versions/locators, what was actually read, unresolved access and the
next useful step. Reuse traceable notes and recheck only changed claims, versions
or time-sensitive facts. Deliver a substantive synthesis, not merely a completed
folder structure.
