# v3.1 Argument And Evidence Contract

Represent the paper as a writing-oriented spine:

research question → gap → contribution chain → mechanism or prediction
→ design/estimand → evidence map → interpretation → scope.

Each claim in the contribution chain needs a section, evidence references, method
dependencies, and a risk level. The object records the author's argument; it does
not invent a contribution.

New or strengthened prose must bind important external claims to a Claim ID/source
locator or carry an explicit limitation marker. Metadata-only and candidate sources
cannot be presented as full-text support. Method writing must state what a design
does not identify.
