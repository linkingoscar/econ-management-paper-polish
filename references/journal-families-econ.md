# Economics Journal Families

Use when the paper targets economics journals or economics-style outlets. This file provides general outlet-family heuristics only. For a named journal, build a `journal-style-card.md` using current guidelines and recent papers.

## General Economics Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Broad research question with relevance beyond a narrow setting.
- Strong identification or theoretical contribution.
- Clear economic mechanism and magnitude.
- Contribution to a major debate or literature.

Writing style:

- Lean introduction.
- Early statement of question, design, and findings.
- Minimal field jargon unless necessary.
- Results framed for a broad economics audience.

Risk:

- Setting too narrow.
- Contribution only incremental.
- Identification not credible enough.

## Applied Micro And Field Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Credible empirical design.
- Careful data construction.
- Field-specific mechanism and policy implications.
- Robustness and heterogeneity grounded in theory.

Writing style:

- More institutional detail than general journals.
- Clear method and identification narrative.
- Contribution to a field-specific literature.

## Labor, Development, Health, Education, Environment, Urban, Trade, IO

Use `subfields-economics.md` for subfield routing.

Common family expectations:

- Field readers know the core debates; avoid overexplaining basics.
- Identify the exact margin of contribution.
- Discuss external validity and mechanism.
- Interpret magnitude in field-relevant units.

## Finance-Economics Boundary Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Market or firm mechanism.
- Identification, alternative explanations, and economic magnitude.
- Institutional setting and market participant behavior.

When the paper is closer to finance than economics, also use finance/accounting packs in `field-style-packs.md`.

## Policy And Public Economics-Oriented Outlets

Typical emphasis:

- Policy design, incidence, welfare, implementation, or institutional implications.
- Credible evidence and transparent assumptions.
- Practical relevance without policy overreach.

Writing style:

- Policy background can appear earlier, but must become an academic question.
- Implications should be scoped to the evidence.

## Chinese Economics CSSCI Journals

Typical emphasis:

- 问题意识.
- 文献缺口.
- 规范的模型与变量说明.
- 稳健性、内生性、机制和异质性分析.
- 政策启示.

Writing style:

- Use `cn-economics-style.md`.
- Keep policy language controlled.
- Contributions should be "边际贡献" with concrete literature mapping.

## Working Paper And Seminar Style

When the user is preparing a working paper or seminar draft:

- Lead with research design and novelty.
- Make assumptions and limitations explicit.
- Surface threats before audience Q&A.
- Use a reviewer-risk list as output.
