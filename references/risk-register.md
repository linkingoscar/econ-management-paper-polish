# Located issues and revision priorities

Use when a substantial review needs an organized issue list. Reuse the existing
revision record; do not maintain duplicate states in a spine, gate report and register.

Record: issue; manuscript/evidence location; why it changes interpretation;
status; feasible remedy; dependency on new data, analysis or sources.

## Priority follows consequence

- **Critical:** a demonstrated defect undermines the central answer or integrity.
- **Major:** a consequential argument, measurement, design or evidence problem.
- **Minor:** a local presentation/clarity issue that does not change the main answer.
- **Optional:** a useful extension whose cost and relevance should be judged.

Severity is not a prediction of rejection. Separate confirmed errors from
unverified concerns, reporting gaps and suggestions as in [quality-gates](quality-gates.md).
Do not infer a severe defect merely because a common test is absent.

## Avoid automatic remedies

- Endogeneity is a problem to diagnose, not an instruction to add IV/DID.
- An unmeasured mechanism can limit a mechanism claim without invalidating a
  well-supported descriptive result.
- Multicollinearity is not automatic permission to remove theoretically necessary
  controls or substitute a predictive estimator.
- Older data or a narrow setting can be appropriate for the question.
- Missing report details do not establish that a procedure was not performed.
- Generic prose should be improved with known specifics, not invented facts.
- Similar findings across specifications do not resolve every causal concern.

Choose among clearer reporting, corrected wording, a narrower supported claim,
additional feasible evidence, or an explicitly different study.
For a new result, update only affected claims through [paper-spine](paper-spine.md).

## Delivery

Lead with the few most consequential findings and their repair order.
State when no material issue was found within the inspected scope.
A checklist total or number of reviewers/agents is not evidence of quality.
