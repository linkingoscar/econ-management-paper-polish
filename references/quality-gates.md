# Proportional quality checks

Use for substantive revision, sensitive passages and manuscript review.
These are checks of the affected work, not mandatory reports or approval gates.
For a line edit, use the preservation check in [style-and-polish](style-and-polish.md).

## Check what could change the conclusion

| Area | Question | Detailed reference if needed |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Did editing change attribution, negation, unit, scope or claim strength? | [Style](style-and-polish.md) |
| Contribution | Does the actual work answer the stated question and support the claimed increment? | [Paper spine](paper-spine.md) |
| Constructs/theory | Is the concept measured/explained as claimed? Are alternatives acknowledged? | [Theory](theory-backing-router.md) |
| Citations | Does inspected content support this exact sentence? What remains unseen? | [Evidence standard](evidence-grading.md) |
| Results | Are scale, comparison, sample, uncertainty and locators accurate? | [Results to text](results-to-text.md) |
| Methods | Are assumptions and actual procedures distinguished from recommendations? | [Methods](empirical-method-router.md) |
| Revision | Does the reply describe edits and analyses that actually exist? | [Revision](revision-matrix.md) |
| Journal/file | Were applicable official rules and final output actually checked? | [Journal](journal-style-card.md) |

Apply research-specific criteria. Do not treat missing directional hypotheses,
fixed effects, mediation or regression tests as defects in all studies.
Methods and data reporting checks do not establish reproducibility; use
[reproducibility-audit](reproducibility-audit.md) to label the actual scope.

## Distinguish finding states

- **Confirmed error:** located conflict or unsupported inference in the material.
- **Reporting gap:** the manuscript does not state something needed to interpret it.
- **Unverified:** necessary evidence/artifact was not inspected or available.
- **Not applicable:** the check does not fit this design or task.
- **Suggestion:** an improvement, with benefit and cost, not a required repair.

"Not reported" does not mean "not performed"; a missing source does not establish
that the claim is false. Flag uncertainty without manufacturing a finding.

## Prioritize and finish

Report consequential problems with location, evidence, effect on interpretation
and a practical correction. Separate changes possible in prose from work needing
new evidence. Do not present checklist totals or simulated reviewer votes as a
quality score or publication prediction.

After an edit, revisit affected claims and dependencies. Repeat broader review
only when new changes or unresolved concerns justify it. If no material issue is
found, say so with the scope checked; do not generate filler criticism.
