# Economics Subfield Router

Use after routing to economics. Select the closest subfield, then combine with `cn-economics-style.md` or `en-economics-style.md`.

## Applied Microeconomics

Routing clues: individuals, firms, households, regions, policies, treatment effects, quasi-experiments.

Writing focus:

- Research question and causal effect.
- Identification and identifying variation.
- Economic magnitude and mechanism.
- External validity and policy relevance.

Reviewer risks:

- Descriptive association framed causally.
- Weak mechanism tests.
- Missing magnitude interpretation.

## Labor, Education, And Health

Routing clues: employment, wages, migration, human capital, schooling, health outcomes, social insurance.

Writing focus:

- Individual/household incentives and institutional setting.
- Selection, sorting, omitted variables, and policy timing.
- Distributional and welfare implications when relevant.

Method sensitivity:

- DID/event study, IV, RD, matching, panel FE, administrative data linkage.

## Development, Rural, And Poverty

Routing clues: rural households, poverty, infrastructure, agriculture, microfinance, development programs.

Writing focus:

- Institutional context and development mechanism.
- Program targeting, selection, spillovers, and external validity.
- Welfare, productivity, and household behavior.

## Environmental, Energy, And Climate Economics

Routing clues: emissions, pollution, energy transition, carbon markets, green finance, environmental regulation.

Writing focus:

- Policy design and environmental-economic tradeoff.
- Firm/household/regional responses.
- Spatial spillovers and measurement of environmental outcomes.
- Mechanism: compliance cost, innovation, relocation, financing, disclosure, productivity.

Method sensitivity:

- DID/event study, spatial models, IV, RD, policy intensity, remote-sensing or monitoring data validation.

## Regional, Urban, And Real Estate Economics

Routing clues: city, region, housing, land, infrastructure, transportation, agglomeration, urban policy.

Writing focus:

- Spatial equilibrium, agglomeration, mobility, local policy, housing/land mechanisms.
- Unit of analysis and spatial spillovers.
- Regional heterogeneity and external validity.

## Industrial Organization And Digital Economy

Routing clues: market power, competition, platforms, pricing, entry, network effects, algorithms, digital markets.

Writing focus:

- Market structure, firm strategy, consumer response, platform rules.
- Competition mechanism and welfare implications.
- Demand/supply or structural assumptions when used.

Method sensitivity:

- Event studies, structural models, platform data, price/quantity response, market definition.

## Public Finance And Taxation

Routing clues: tax policy, fiscal transfer, government spending, subsidies, public goods, local finance.

Writing focus:

- Incentives of taxpayers, firms, and governments.
- Fiscal federalism, enforcement, tax incidence, behavioral response.
- Policy variation and institutional detail.

## International Trade And Globalization

Routing clues: exports, imports, tariffs, exchange rates, trade shocks, global value chains, FDI.

Writing focus:

- Trade exposure and firm/worker/region response.
- Shock construction and identification.
- Reallocation, productivity, employment, and welfare.

## Macro, Monetary, And Financial Economics

Routing clues: inflation, monetary policy, credit cycles, macro shocks, expectations, systemic risk.

Writing focus:

- Aggregate mechanism and transmission channel.
- Time-series or macro-panel identification.
- Calibration/structural assumptions if applicable.

## Political Economy And Institutional Economics

Routing clues: governance, corruption, elections, institutions, regulation, political connections.

Writing focus:

- Institutional mechanism and incentives.
- Exogenous political or institutional variation.
- Alternative explanations around selection and omitted institutions.

## Chinese Economics Subfield Note

For Chinese CSSCI economics papers:

- Make the China-specific policy or institutional setting concrete.
- Translate policy background into a research question.
- Keep "政策启示" evidence-bound.
- Cite official documents for policy facts and verified academic sources for theory/method claims.
