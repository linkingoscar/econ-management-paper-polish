# Management Journal Families

Use relevant portions for management or related audiences. These are editorial heuristics, not verified universal outlet expectations. Hypothesis-testing, theory-building, descriptive and qualitative work need different structures. For named-journal compliance use [journal-style-card](journal-style-card.md): official instructions establish requirements; sample articles only illustrate conventions.

## General Management And Organization Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Strong theoretical puzzle.
- Construct precision.
- Mechanism and boundary conditions.
- Contribution to named theory conversations.
- Evidence that returns to theory.

Writing style:

- Theory-driven introduction.
- Clear questions, hypotheses, propositions or process explanations as appropriate to the study.
- Discussion organized by theoretical contributions.

Risk:

- Empirical effect with insufficient theory.
- New context only, no theory extension.
- Managerial implication too generic.

## Strategy Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Firm heterogeneity, competitive advantage, strategic choices, governance, resources, capabilities, ecosystems.
- Mechanisms linking strategy to performance, innovation, survival, or value capture.
- Boundary conditions across industries, institutions, uncertainty, or resources.

Writing style:

- Clear level of analysis.
- Strategic tradeoff and mechanism.
- Contribution to strategy conversation, not just firm-level evidence.

## OB And HR Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Psychological, social, or behavioral mechanisms.
- Construct validity and measurement.
- Multilevel theory when relevant.
- Common-method and causal-limit handling.

Writing style:

- Theory and hypotheses carry much of the contribution.
- Results should interpret construct relationships, not only coefficients.

## Organization Theory And Sociology-Oriented Management

Typical emphasis:

- Institutional, network, identity, category, routine, legitimacy, or field-level theory.
- Mechanism, process, and theoretical abstraction.
- Often accepts qualitative or mixed evidence when theoretically deep.

Writing style:

- Less regression-table centric.
- More conceptual and process-oriented.

## Marketing Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Consumer mechanism or marketing decision relevance.
- Behavioral validity, experimental design, or quantitative model rigor.
- Managerial actionability linked to evidence.

Writing style:

- For consumer behavior: theory and experiments.
- For quantitative marketing: model, identification, prediction/decision relevance.

## Information Systems Journals

Typical emphasis:

- IT artifact, digital affordance, platform mechanism, algorithm/data system, sociotechnical theory, or economics of digital systems.
- The digital context must create theoretical, design, or empirical value.

Writing style:

- Choose a route: behavioral IS, economics of IS, design science, digital strategy, platform empirics.
- Avoid generic "digital transformation improves performance" writing.

## Operations And Supply Chain Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Operational process, supply-chain coordination, service operations, resilience, efficiency, quality, or decision models.
- Practical decision relevance and methodological rigor.

Writing style:

- Process and mechanism clarity.
- Data/optimization/model assumptions when relevant.

## Chinese Management CSSCI Journals

Typical emphasis:

- 管理问题意识.
- 与研究方式相称的理论、过程解释或研究假设.
- 规范实证或调查/实验设计.
- 理论贡献和管理启示.

Writing style:

- Use `cn-management-style.md`.
- Avoid pure policy commentary unless public management.
- Keep "管理启示" actionable and evidence-bound.

## English Management Field Journals

Typical emphasis:

- Theoretical contribution and fit to the journal's conversation.
- Style varies by journal and article type; use actual instructions and relevant samples for close adaptation.

Writing style:

- Use `en-management-style.md`.
- Build a named-journal style card for close adaptation.
