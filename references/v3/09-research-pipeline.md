# Research Pipeline Pack

## Eight stages

1. topic and contribution diagnosis;
2. literature search and evidence ledger;
3. theory/mechanism and predictions;
4. research design and estimand;
5. manuscript drafting;
6. deterministic self-audit;
7. reviewer-style audit;
8. revision, journal adaptation, and submission handoff.

Each stage has an entry condition, output artifact, and exit gate. Do not advance a
paper with an unresolved blocking risk merely because prose is polished. The handoff
should include the routing card, paper state, evidence pack, journal card, revision
matrix, and audit report when applicable.

## Delegation contract

Subtasks may be parallelized only when inputs and outputs are explicit: literature
search, method diagnosis, style polish, evidence audit, and reviewer simulation. A
single-threaded environment uses the same step order serially. Agents must not invent
sources, duplicate route inference, or overwrite the manuscript without a diff and
protected-token check.
