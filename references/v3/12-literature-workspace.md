# Literature Workspace Pack

## Workspace artifacts

- research question and subquestions;
- paper pool with core, extended, and peripheral tiers;
- timeline, method map, construct/variable map, and gap matrix;
- close-reading note for each paper: question, theory, data, design, result,
  limitation, and relevance to the current claim.

Paper-pool status is not evidence grade. A core paper can still be candidate-only for
a particular sentence. Link close-reading notes to Claim IDs and source URLs; keep
the original bibliographic record intact.

## Survey output

Organize a survey by debates or unresolved questions, not a chronological list. For
each stream report consensus, disagreement, design differences, and the precise gap
the manuscript addresses. Mark frontier claims with checked dates and access limits.

When no local index or database is available, use a user-guided or metadata-only
workspace and say so; do not fabricate a “complete” literature review.
