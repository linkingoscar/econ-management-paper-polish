# Worked writing examples

Use the closest example for a concrete passage, not the whole file by default.
All studies and numbers below are synthetic teaching material. Do not copy their
findings into a user's paper or count these examples as external validation data.
The same topic can require different prose across outlets; the moves are optional.

Examples 1–4 and 8 preserve the supplied substantive content. Examples 5–7 propose
changes to claim strength or implication scope: explain these changes, follow the
user's existing revision authorization, and do not silently treat them as polish.
Keep references attached to the claims they support in every example.

## 1. 中文经济学：把引言开头落到具体问题

原文：在数字经济蓬勃发展的时代背景下，平台佣金具有重要意义。本文关注小商户
是否将佣金变化转嫁给消费者，并使用商户层面的月度价格资料考察这一问题。

改文：平台佣金变化是否会传导至消费者价格？本文使用商户层面的月度价格资料，
考察小商户对佣金变化的价格反应。

理由：删去没有承载研究信息的背景句，保留对象、问题和资料。原文没有交代识别
设计，不能自行补成“利用外生冲击识别因果效应”。后续贡献段需要已有研究和实际
发现；仅凭这个开头不能生成“首次证明”或一组虚构文献。

## 2. English economics: explain what the paper asks

Before: Housing access is an important and complex topic. This paper uses linked
application records to study whether longer processing times are associated with
applicant withdrawal. We examine differences by household income.

After: Are longer housing-application processing times associated with applicant
withdrawal? We study this relationship using linked application records and examine
how it varies with household income.

Why: The question and data now carry the opening. The association and the income
comparison remain; neither has become an identified treatment effect. Do not add
a headline estimate or an explanation of the income pattern until supplied.

## 3. 中文经济学：不显著结果也需要准确解释

原文：表3的估计系数为0.04，95%置信区间为[-0.03, 0.11]，结果不显著。当前结果
尚不能排除正向或负向关系，因此不能据此认定不存在关系。

改文：表3报告的估计系数为0.04，95%置信区间为[-0.03, 0.11]，统计上不显著。
该区间同时包含负值和正值，现有估计不能据此确定关系的方向，也不能认定不存在关系。

理由：保留表号、点估计、区间和不确定性，解释精度而不是把“不显著”变成“无影响”。
是否能排除具有实际意义的效应，还需要用户提供有依据的比较标准。

## 4. English economics: magnitude needs a supplied scale

Before: Table 2 reports a coefficient of 0.02 on the binary program indicator in
a linear probability model of employment, relative to a comparison mean of 0.40.
This is a difference of 2 percentage points, or 5 percent of the comparison mean.
The estimate is conditional on the stated identifying assumptions.

After: In Table 2, the coefficient on the program indicator is 0.02. In this linear
probability model, it corresponds to a difference of 2 percentage points, or
5 percent of the comparison mean of 0.40. This interpretation remains conditional
on the stated identifying assumptions.

Why: All inputs to the magnitude calculation were supplied: model, regressor scale,
coefficient, and comparison mean. A coefficient of 0.02 in a logit model would not
license the same conversion. Keep the supplied uncertainty estimate nearby if
available; do not invent a standard error to complete the paragraph.

## 5. 中文管理学：机制段区分理论路径与识别

原文：资源获取理论认为，外部合作可能扩大企业的信息渠道。表4中合作强度与
信息获取、信息获取与创新产出均呈正相关，因此我们证明了信息获取的因果中介机制。

建议改文：资源获取理论提示，外部合作可能通过拓宽信息渠道与创新产出形成联系。
表4中合作强度与信息获取、信息获取与创新产出的正相关关系，与这一理论路径一致。
这些关联证据尚不足以单独识别信息获取的因果中介作用。

理由：说明哪个行为过程连接构念，并把表中关联证据放在合适的位置。“证明机制”
被降为“与路径一致”，属于需要说明的实质修改。进一步判断应查看时序、测量和
混淆处理，不能凭两个显著系数补写中介识别已经成立。

## 6. English management: theorize the process without claiming proof

Before: Psychological safety means that team members feel able to take interpersonal
risks. We argue that it may help employees speak up because disagreement feels less
costly. Our cross-sectional survey proves this causal mechanism.

Suggested revision: Psychological safety may make speaking up less interpersonally
costly by reducing the perceived risk of disagreement. This argument links the
team climate to an employee's decision to voice concerns. The cross-sectional
survey can examine associations relevant to this account; it does not by itself
establish the proposed causal mechanism.

Why: The mechanism now names an actor, a perceived cost, and a behavioral response.
The definition and proposed process come from the supplied text. The last sentence
changes the claim strength and must be explained as a substantive recommendation.
Do not invent a mediator measure or a significant path to complete the theory.

## 7. 中文管理学：讨论和启示回到研究范围

原文：本研究访谈了12家制造企业，发现受访企业在需求波动时会重新协商供应协议。
因此，所有企业都应采用这种做法，这丰富了相关研究。

建议改文：对12家制造企业的访谈表明，受访企业会通过重新协商供应协议应对需求波动。
这一发现提供了理解样本企业如何调整合作安排的过程线索。其对其他行业的适用性
仍需进一步研究，不能据此提出面向所有企业的统一建议。

理由：保留案例数、行为和情境，收窄没有证据支持的普遍建议。理论贡献还需要对照
明确的理论观点或既有解释，不能将“提供过程线索”包装成已经推进某一理论。

## 8. English management: keep a discussion contribution specific

Before: This study contributes to the literature on employee voice. Our findings
suggest that the association between manager openness and voice varies with task
interdependence. The study covers project teams in one organization and cannot
establish whether this pattern holds in other settings.

After: Our findings suggest that task interdependence conditions the association
between manager openness and employee voice. This pattern directs attention to the
work context in discussions of voice. Because the study covers project teams in
one organization, its applicability to other settings remains unresolved.

Why: The discussion states the supplied insight and boundary rather than relying
on a generic contribution claim. “Conditions the association” does not establish
a causal moderating effect. Further theoretical positioning needs the actual
literature comparison; a new sample alone does not establish a theoretical advance.

## Translation and output example

Source (the manuscript explicitly defines amounts in RMB): 样本企业总资产为1.5亿元，
员工持股比例提高2个百分点。上述变化不能证明因果关系。

Target: The sample firm's total assets were RMB 150 million, and its employee
ownership share increased by 2 percentage points. These changes do not establish
a causal relationship.

Check: 1.5亿元 = RMB 150 million; 2个百分点 = 2 percentage points, not 2 percent.
Keep the negation and the scope of the final sentence. The numeric equivalence
screen checks quantities only; it cannot verify those semantic decisions.
If the source instead says “样本企业平均总资产”, the target must retain “average”.

For Word-oriented output, the target paragraph above is sufficient. Do not wrap
it in a code block or add a bilingual comparison table unless requested.
In LaTeX, a supplied citation such as \citep{author2020} stays attached to its claim,
and a plain-text percentage such as 5% becomes 5\% without changing math content.
