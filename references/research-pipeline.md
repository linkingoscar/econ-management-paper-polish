# Optional research project workflow

Use only for extended planning or a request to organize work from an idea toward
a manuscript. Enter at the user's current task; existing work need not repeat
earlier stages. Writing can begin before research ends if planned and observed
content remain clearly distinguished.

## Work from available artifacts

| Work area | Useful input | Honest output |
|---|---|---|
| Question and feasibility | Idea/draft, actual data or access constraints | Focused question and feasible options |
| Literature positioning | Relevant accessible sources | Known evidence, disagreements, scoped contribution |
| Reasoning and design | Constructs, question, evidence plan | Theory/process/model or design appropriate to the research approach |
| Evidence acquisition / analysis / verification | Actual observations, data, code, proofs, qualitative materials or supplied outputs | Inspected results and their limits, or explicit unresolved work |
| Writing and integration | Material adequate for each requested section | Draft connected to actual evidence; provisional parts marked |
| Review and correction | Draft plus relevant evidence | Located issues and completed feasible repairs |
| Journal/file preparation | Target, article type, stage, current rules | Editable deliverables and stated checks |
| Reviewer response, when applicable | Real comments and actual revisions | Truthful responses tied to manuscript changes |

The evidence/analysis step is not implied by a completed design.
If the user requests actual analysis and tools/materials permit it, execute and
inspect outputs; otherwise identify what the author must supply or do.
No estimated effects, successful tests, interview quotes or completed proofs may
be invented to advance the workflow.

## Organize without bureaucracy

Use [paper-spine](paper-spine.md) for the argument,
[evidence workflow](evidence-citation-workflow.md) for sources,
[results-to-text](results-to-text.md) for results and
[revision-matrix](revision-matrix.md) for actual revisions.

Keep a short artifact/task record only when needed across sessions:
what exists; what was inspected/performed; unresolved dependency; next useful action.
Do not require every possible deliverable or repeated stage approval.
A paragraph request should not activate the whole workflow.

For descriptive, theoretical, predictive or qualitative work, replace causal and
hypothesis-testing tasks with the appropriate reasoning. Absence of directional
hypotheses is not a universal gap.

## Review and finish

Use [stage checks](pipeline-stage-gates.md) to avoid unsupported transitions.
A simulated review is optional and is not external peer review or an acceptance
forecast. [Delegation](pipeline-delegation.md) is conditional on the task and host.
First submission does not require a response letter.
Report what is ready for the requested next use and what remains unverified.
