# Evidence-dependent transitions

Use within [research-pipeline](research-pipeline.md) for extended tasks.
These checks guide what can be claimed or delivered, not a sequence of approval
forms. Do not require the user to "accept responsibility" to receive partial work.

| Transition | Check | If material is missing |
|---|---|---|
| Idea → research plan | Question and feasible evidence are specified enough to plan | Offer conditional options; ask only consequential questions |
| Sources → literature claim | Actual inspected sources support its scope | Narrow the claim or label the open literature question |
| Reasoning → design | The design can address the question under stated conditions | Explain the gap without forcing a hypothesis/causal template |
| Design → results narrative | Actual results/evidence have been supplied or produced and inspected | Write methods/plan; leave findings explicitly unknown |
| Result → abstract/conclusion | Units, comparison, uncertainty and scope support the statement | Correct or qualify only what the evidence resolves |
| Planned revision → completed reply | The change and its evidence actually exist | Keep response provisional; separate pending actions |
| Manuscript → journal/file deliverable | Applicable official rules and requested file checks were performed | Deliver useful draft/source; identify unchecked items |

"Pass" never means scientifically valid or accepted for publication.
A user may choose a provisional draft or skip an optional review. That does not
turn missing results, unseen sources or unperformed checks into verified facts.

Do not require a full literature review before editing an introduction, a mechanism
test for every study, or a response letter at initial submission.
Use [quality-gates](quality-gates.md) for the affected content and
[reproducibility-audit](reproducibility-audit.md) for the exact verification scope.
