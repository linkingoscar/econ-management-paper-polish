# Management Subfield Router

Use after routing to management or broader business-school fields. Select the closest subfield, then combine with `cn-management-style.md` or `en-management-style.md`.

## Strategic Management

Routing clues: competitive advantage, performance, diversification, alliances, governance, capabilities, resources, ecosystems.

Writing focus:

- Firm heterogeneity, strategic choice, capability, governance, or competitive positioning.
- Mechanisms that connect strategy to performance or survival.
- Boundary conditions around industry, uncertainty, resources, or institutions.

Reviewer risks:

- Strategic construct not distinguished from generic firm behavior.
- Performance effect without theory.
- Post-hoc heterogeneity.

## Organization Theory

Routing clues: institutions, legitimacy, routines, organizational forms, categories, identity, networks, adaptation.

Writing focus:

- Theoretical puzzle and construct precision.
- Process, mechanisms, and boundary conditions.
- Organization-level or field-level implications.

Reviewer risks:

- Too empirical with insufficient theory.
- Context does not alter or extend theory.

## OB And HRM

Routing clues: employees, leadership, teams, motivation, commitment, turnover, creativity, HR practices.

Writing focus:

- Psychological or social mechanism.
- Construct validity and measurement.
- Multilevel logic if individuals are nested in teams/firms.
- Common-method bias and causal limits.

## Innovation And Entrepreneurship

Routing clues: R&D, patents, innovation performance, startups, entrepreneurial orientation, venture financing, ecosystems.

Writing focus:

- Knowledge, uncertainty, resources, institutions, entrepreneurial action.
- Distinguish innovation input, process, output, and impact.
- For entrepreneurship, clarify venture stage and actor.

## Corporate Governance And ESG In Management

Routing clues: boards, executives, ownership, governance mechanisms, CSR/ESG, stakeholders.

Writing focus:

- Agency, stakeholder, legitimacy, resource dependence, upper-echelons, or institutional mechanisms.
- Distinguish management-theory route from finance route.
- Avoid overbroad ESG normativity without mechanism.

## Marketing And Consumer Research

Routing clues: consumers, brands, advertising, channels, pricing, online reviews, customer behavior.

Writing focus:

- Consumer psychology or firm marketing decision.
- Construct validity for behavioral work.
- Decision relevance and market response for quantitative marketing.

## Information Systems And Digital Transformation

Routing clues: IT adoption, platforms, AI, algorithms, digital transformation, data governance, online communities.

Writing focus:

- Technology artifact or digital affordance.
- Sociotechnical, behavioral, economics-of-IS, or design-science route.
- Digital context should create theory or identification value.

## Operations And Supply Chain Management

Routing clues: supply chain, logistics, production, inventory, service operations, resilience, quality.

Writing focus:

- Operational mechanism, process, coordination, risk, resilience, or efficiency.
- Data/process validity and managerial decision relevance.
- Distinguish operational performance from generic firm performance.

## Public Management And Policy Management

Routing clues: government agencies, public service, governance, bureaucrats, regulation, public organizations.

Writing focus:

- Governance capacity, incentives, accountability, implementation, public value.
- Institutional setting and policy process.
- Avoid treating public-sector implications as firm management advice.

## Tourism, Hospitality, And Service Management

Routing clues: tourism destinations, hotels, service experience, visitors, hospitality firms.

Writing focus:

- Service experience, destination governance, consumer behavior, platform reviews, sustainability.
- Industry-specific mechanism and external validity.

## Chinese Management Subfield Note

For Chinese CSSCI management papers:

- Problem consciousness must become a theoretical question.
- Use "管理启示" only after theory and evidence are clear.
- Chinese institutional context can be valuable, but not automatically a contribution.
